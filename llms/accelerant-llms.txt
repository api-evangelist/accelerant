# Accelerant

> Accelerant Holdings (NYSE: ARX) operates the Accelerant Risk Exchange, a data-driven marketplace connecting underwriters of specialty insurance risk — MGAs, program administrators, captive managers and retail brokers, known as Members — with risk capital providers including insurers, reinsurers and institutional investors. Founded in 2018 and headquartered in Grand Cayman, Accelerant runs Exchange Services, MGA Operations and Underwriting segments across the US, Canada, the UK, Europe and Australia.

## APIs

- [Accelerant Risk Exchange Platform](https://docs.accelerant.ai/): The member-facing Accelerant Risk Exchange platform and its supporting API surface. Base URL https://api.accelerant.ai. Accelerant states that its risk indices plug directly into Member underwriting and policy administration systems via API. Both the API host and the developer documentation are gated to Members.

## Platform capabilities

- Risk scoring — AI-driven real-time feedback on risk quality for specialty underwriters
- Portfolio risk monitoring — identifying unexpected variance across exchange premium
- Geospatial intelligence
- Product intelligence
- Underwriting referrals
- Bordereaux (BDX) submissions, data corrections and observability
- Solutions exchange, reinsurance broker and surety tooling
- Insurer data products and external analytics
